## Understand it first (before you do anything)
**What is it?** Three ways to organise agents: one agent with tools; a team where a leader delegates to members; a workflow with fixed steps. Choose the simplest that works.

**How does it work?** agent: one loop. team: leader decides which member to call and combines results. workflow: step 1 > step 2 > step 3, each step an agent or function.

**Answer these in your `ACTIVITY_LOG.md` before you start the steps:**
- What does each cost (tokens, steps, latency)?
- Which is easiest to test and why?
- Which gives the most control?
